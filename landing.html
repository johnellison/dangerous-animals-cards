Why This Exists

A dad who loves animals built this game for his kids — ages 4 and 7 — so they could play together even when he is far away.

Every card is a chance to discover something wild. Every guess is a little victory. And every animal collected is a memory they share, no matter the distance.

No accounts. No ads. No tracking. Just a game made with love.

Three Ways to Play

Discover

Swipe through beautiful animal cards and tap to flip. Learn fun facts about every creature.

Guess

Photo quiz with streaks and spaced repetition. The game remembers what you know.

Collect

Track your progress across 10 categories. Discover every animal from snakes to sharks.
